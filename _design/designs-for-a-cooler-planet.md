description: Introducing letterpress printing technology to the Arabic script
  stripped the writing system of many of its characteristics. The loss of
  multiple integral script qualities trickled down to modern standards of Arabic
  font building and typesetting, becoming part of modern typographic norms. One
  forgotten or lost characteristic of the Arabic script is the agency given to
  its writers to control the placement, shape, and composition of its
  diacritics, using it to manage the negative spaces or to create diacritical
  compositions depending solely on the scribe’s aesthetic tastes. This research
  studies and attempts to revive these agencies through accessible modes of font
  building, producing functional experimental fonts usable in mainstream
  typesetting environments.
