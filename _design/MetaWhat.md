description: |-
  MetaWhat is a variable bilingual typeface. It is inspired by the idea that the metaverse is an internet that is accessed using the physical sense of space, and that the metaverse allows for shapeshifting. Available with Latin and Arabic alphabets, this variable typeface is scalable in weight and form. This project was designed with Khajag Apelian for Studio Sharaf.

  This typeface won a Certificate of Typographic Excellence fromthe <a href="[https://www.oneclub.org/awards/tdcawards/-award/53821/metawhata/](https://www.oneclub.org/awards/tdcawards/-award/53821/metawhata/)" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Type Directors Club NYC</a>.
