description: >-
  A reimagining of Paul Felton's book The Ten Commandments of Typography: Type
  Heresy as an actual religious manuscript. The commandments are identically and
  traditionally typeset and each followed by its corresponding sin. The sins are
  set against red backgrounds and concealed in French folds, so readers have to
  kill (sin) the book and make it bleed to read the heresies.


  This project passed the 2016 ISTD Student Assessment with Merit.
