description: >-
  MalignBenign is an Experimental Electronic EP by the Lebanese music producer
  Liliane Chlela. The album cover art had to match the album's name, feel, and
  music. The design was made and inspired by microscopic cancer cell imagery.
  The words Malign and Benign were formed from manipulated cells and dissolved
  into gritty grains and pixels, reflecting the album's underground,
  experimental nature.


  Cells adapted from NCI / Dr. Lance Liotta Lab microscopic slides (Public
  Domain)
