description: >-
  Directly translated, the Arabic lettering reads, consciousness/awareness is
  seafoam [it is ever-changing]. The events that struck the world and
  specifically my country, Lebanon, in 2020, have shown me that what we
  individually, or in some cases collectively, perceive as reality can change in
  a heartbeat. The visual is ever-changing [animatic] and mimics the foamy
  residue left by the sea's tides, ever-changing and unstable.


  It started with the base lettering, which I drew with high contrast and
  drastic boldness, strongly influenced by the Ruqua style, creating a strong
  anchor/base for the poster(s). I then manually treated this lettering with ink
  bubbles to achieve a seafoam oceanic effect. I produced various renditions of
  this lettering, each with a different colour treatment, all reminiscent of the
  ocean, to highlight the topic's ever-changing nature. I also animated a poster
  to capture the spirit of the sea and reinforce the idea of constant change,
  even in a finished product.
