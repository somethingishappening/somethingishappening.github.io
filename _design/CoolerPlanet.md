description: >-
  Designs for a Cooler Planet is an annual exhibition at Aalto University that
  showcases forward-thinking projects and is part of Helsinki Design Week. The
  theme for 2022 was “Life 1.5”, and the visual identity needed to reflect what
  a hypothetical but very probable Life 1.5 way of life could look like and
  embody. 


  Although depictions of the future can appear scary and bleak in media and
  science fiction novels, movies, and television series, we intended our project
  to show a beautiful, abundant, and hopeful 1.5 lifestyle. We embraced the
  ideals of repurposing, remixing, and building upon existing infrastructure and
  materials; those ideals became the core of our concept, its aesthetics, and
  its limitations. These core ideas helped us convey that sustainability does
  not mean scarcity and that hidden potential and beauty can exist in what
  people consider waste. We built the exhibition’s visual identity and meeting
  point using cardboard found around the university. We covered the branding and
  text on the cardboard with a white layer, then printed our design on top,
  embracing the accidental graphic elements and layout effects that emerged
  through this process. The unit was meant to showcase possibilities and radical
  thinking in approaching future sustainable design and creativity. 


  This project was designed in collaboration with [Milja
  Komulainen](https://www.miljakomulainen.com/about) and [Baldur
  Heraldson](www.fiffstudio.com) (Architect).
