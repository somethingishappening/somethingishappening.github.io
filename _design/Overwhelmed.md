  Overwhelmed visualises thought triggers in an oversaturated medium/mental
  state. One aim of this project was to let letters contribute to the overall
  meaning and context of the text. The project visualises [MY] thought triggers
  in an oversaturated medium/mental state and explores the limitations and
  boundaries of letterforms. The intention is to typographically visualise my
  personal experience and interpretation of overthinking, anxiety, panic
  attacks, and external triggers by abstractly demonstrating a mind taken over
  by stimulants and overwhelmed to the point of suffocation/panic attack. The
  imagery and letters picture intrusive thoughts and ideas as they mingle and
  transform, having a consciousness of their own, growing and merging with
  others, forming a giant mass, and taking over a mind already saturated with
  anxiety. 


  Done by triggering [external stimulants] supersaturated Sodium acetate
  solution [an overcrowded mind] at certain points to create letterforms. The
  crystal letters expand from single points and merge with others until the
  basic letter shape is completely engulfed/consumed in the crystalline
  structures, depicting rising intrusive thoughts taking over an oversaturated
  mind, the Petri dish. 


  This project was published in Slanted Magazine's Experimental Type 3.0
  publication.
